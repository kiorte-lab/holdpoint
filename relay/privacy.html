Holdpoint privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Holdpoint lets you answer your coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot CLI, Antigravity CLI) from a Garmin watch or a phone. This page explains what the Holdpoint watch app, the holdpoint command-line tool and the Holdpoint relay handle, and what they don't.

What goes through the relay

The relay passes messages between the computer running your agent and your watch or phone. It handles:

  • Approval requests and "finished" cards: the agent's name, the tool it wants to use, a short summary and up to 2,000 characters of detail (for example the command it wants to run, or the end of its last message), the folder it is working in, and the name you gave your computer.
  • Your answers: allow, deny, or the reply you sent from the watch.
  • Pairing records: a random ID and a hashed access token for each watch, phone and computer you pair, the names you give them, your Away setting, your reply buttons, and your ntfy topic if you set one.

How long it is kept

  • Requests and replies are held in memory only. Unanswered ones expire after their time limit (at most one hour), and answered ones are deleted within an hour. They are never written to disk or to a database.
  • A computer's pairing record is kept until you unpair it (holdpoint unpair, or Unpair on the phone page). A watch's record (a random ID, a token hash and your settings) is kept while you use the app.
  • Six-digit pairing codes are kept in memory and expire after ten minutes.

What Holdpoint doesn't collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number.
  • No location, health or fitness data from your watch.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no cookies.
  • Your code and files stay on your computer. Only the short summaries described above leave it.

Who can see it

Only the devices you paired can read your requests; every call needs a secret token, and the relay stores only a hash of it. Nothing is sold or shared. The public relay runs on Cloudflare Workers, which processes the traffic on our behalf; Cloudflare's own privacy policy covers their infrastructure. Connections use HTTPS.

Optional phone notifications (ntfy)

If you set an ntfy topic, the relay sends a notification to ntfy.sh containing only "Approval waiting" or "Agent finished", the tool name and your computer's name. No command text or code is included. Leave the topic empty to turn this off.

Running your own relay

The relay is open source. You can run it on your own server and point Holdpoint at it with holdpoint pair --relay <your URL>, in which case none of your data passes through the public relay.

Removing your data

Run holdpoint unpair on each computer, or use Unpair on the phone page; that deletes the computer's record. Uninstalling the watch app removes everything stored on the watch. To have a watch's record deleted from the relay, email support@kiorte.com with the watch's pairing code and it will be removed.
